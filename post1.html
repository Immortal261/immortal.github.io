Motorcycles

My Life with Motorcycles.

For me, motorcycles are more than just vehicles. They are freedom, adrenaline, technology and a completely different way of experiencing the road.

How It Started

My interest in motorcycles started with curiosity. At first, I was mostly interested in how motorcycles looked, sounded and behaved on the road.

Eventually, I decided that simply watching motorcycles was not enough. I wanted to understand what it actually feels like to ride. That decision completely changed my relationship with motorcycles.

My first motorcycle was a KTM RC125. It was a relatively small bike, but it gave me my first real experience with riding and helped me understand how much I enjoy being on two wheels.

"The road feels completely different when you experience it on a motorcycle."

My Current Motorcycle

Today I ride a CFMOTO 675SR-R. It is a much more powerful and serious motorcycle compared with my first bike.

One of the things I like most about it is the combination of performance and design. The motorcycle looks aggressive, but it is also interesting from a technical perspective.

Why I Like Riding

The biggest reason is the feeling of freedom. When you ride a motorcycle, you are much more connected to your surroundings than when you travel inside a car.

You notice the weather, the road surface, the temperature and the environment around you. Even a normal trip across the city can feel like a small adventure.

I also enjoy the process of learning. There is always something new to understand about motorcycles, riding technique, maintenance and equipment.

What Motorcycles Teach Me

  • Patience and concentration.
  • Responsibility for my own decisions.
  • Understanding how important preparation is.
  • Respect for the road and other people.
  • How to stay calm in unexpected situations.

Motorcycles and Traveling

Another reason I love motorcycles is that they make travelling more interesting. A long-distance motorcycle trip is not only about reaching the destination.

The road itself becomes part of the journey. Mountains, small towns, different landscapes and unexpected stops can become some of the best parts of the trip.

Future Plans

I want to continue improving as a rider and explore more roads. In the future, I would like to combine motorcycles and travelling by taking longer trips and discovering new countries.

For me, motorcycles are not simply about speed. They are about experiences, freedom and the feeling that there is always another road to explore.
