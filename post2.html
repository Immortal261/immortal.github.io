About Me

A Little About Me.

I am a university student who enjoys discovering new things, meeting people, travelling and collecting experiences. This page is a small introduction to who I am outside of university.

Who am I?

It is always difficult to describe yourself in a few sentences. People are much more complicated than a list of interests or hobbies. Still, I think my personality can be described by curiosity and the desire to experience something new.

I do not like staying in one place for too long. I enjoy changing my environment, discovering new places and learning from different experiences. Sometimes this can be something big, like travelling to another country. Other times it can be something simple, like discovering a new road on a motorcycle.

"The best memories usually come from experiences you did not plan."

University Life

University is an important part of my everyday life. It is not only about lectures, assignments and exams. It is also a period when you learn how to manage your time, communicate with different people and make your own decisions.

Balancing university with personal interests can sometimes be difficult. There are days when I have a lot of things to do, and there are also days when I simply want to take a break and do something I enjoy.

I think this balance is important because life should not consist only of studying or working. Personal interests and experiences make everyday life more interesting.

Things I Enjoy

There are several things that usually make my free time more interesting:

  • Riding motorcycles and exploring new roads.
  • Travelling and discovering new places.
  • Spending time with friends.
  • Listening to music.
  • Learning something new when I become interested in a topic.
  • Trying new experiences instead of doing the same thing every day.

My Personality

I would describe myself as curious and independent. When something catches my attention, I usually want to understand it better and try it myself.

At the same time, I appreciate simple things. A good conversation, a late evening ride, a new place or a spontaneous plan can easily become one of my favourite memories.

I also think that people change a lot over time. Because of that, this website is not meant to be a perfect description of me. It is simply a snapshot of my life and the things I currently care about.

Why I Created This Blog

I created this website as a personal space where I can collect my interests and experiences. Instead of keeping everything only in my phone or in my memory, I wanted to have a small place on the internet that represents my current life.

The blog is mainly about three things: motorcycles, myself and travelling. These topics are different, but together they describe a big part of how I spend my time and what I find interesting.
